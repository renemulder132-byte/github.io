Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Orvo is a personal home assistant used only by its owner. This policy explains how Orvo handles Google user data.

What Orvo accesses

  • Gmail: reading messages and sending a message after the owner confirms it.
  • Google Calendar: reading events and creating an event after the owner confirms it.

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer the owner's own questions, such as "do I have new mail" or "what is on my calendar today". Orvo does not delete mail or events.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties. To answer a typed question, a short excerpt may be sent to the AI service the owner uses; it is not stored there for training.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Orvo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
